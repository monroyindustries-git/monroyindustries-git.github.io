About Monroy Industries

Francisco Monroy, Founder & CEO

Monroy Industries is an independent technology studio building mobile applications, web products, and software ventures with the potential to scale into standalone companies. I founded it in 2024 after leaving my Ph.D. program in electrical engineering, where I developed an AI-driven wound analysis application for healthcare, to pursue a faster, more direct path to building impactful technology.

Our long-term focus spans climate tech, space exploration, biotechnology, cybersecurity, energy, and advanced manufacturing, guided by a commitment to bold, transformative technology with the potential for meaningful, lasting impact.
